User Guide

Everything you need to browse, read, and manage your digib00age library.

digib00age is your personal, self-hosted comic library — it scans a folder of CBZ/CBR files on your own network and turns them into a browsable, searchable library with reading progress, ratings, and metadata you control. This guide covers everything the app can do, organized into three areas:

Library

Browsing your collection, reading an issue, organizing with favourites, flags, and ratings, and acting on several issues at once.

Admin

Setting up scan folders, backups, appearance, and the pre-ingest Processing Tools — everything involved in running your own server.

Editor

Two ways to fix or fill in a comic's metadata: a quick single-issue Basic Editor, and a batch-tagging Full Editor for staging files before they join your library.

Tip

New here? Start with Library to learn your way around, then come back to Admin once you're ready to add your own comics.
